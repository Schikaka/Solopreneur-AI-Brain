Build a professional SaaS landing page for NarrativeAI, a privacy-first AI reporting assistant for boutique marketing agencies.

This prompt is intended for the Emergent App Builder.

Goal:
Convert agency owners and account directors into first paid customers or demo bookings.

Audience:
Boutique agencies with 5-30 employees that manage paid media, SEO, lifecycle marketing, or performance reporting for multiple clients.

Positioning:
NarrativeAI turns marketing CSV exports into boardroom-ready client report narratives in under a minute. It is not another dashboard. It is the missing narrative layer between raw campaign data and client communication.

Hero:
Headline: Client-ready marketing report narratives in 60 seconds.
Subheadline: Upload a CSV, choose the strategic goal, and generate a polished executive summary your agency can send to clients the same day.
Primary CTA: Upgrade to Elite
Secondary CTA: Watch 60-second demo

Visual direction:
Use a clean B2B SaaS style with a calm white and light-blue interface, sharp typography, professional spacing, and a product-first hero showing a report builder UI. Avoid cartoon illustrations. Make the first viewport clearly show NarrativeAI as the product.

Core sections:
1. Problem section: agencies already have the numbers, but account managers still spend hours writing the explanation.
2. Workflow section: upload CSV, select directive, generate narrative, refine, save locally.
3. Privacy section: raw CSVs are processed in memory, reports can be saved in the browser's Local History Vault, and client data stays under agency control.
4. Output section: three executive insights, achievement-based framing, boardroom narrative, fact-checked recommendations.
5. Use cases: monthly client reports, pre-call performance summaries, budget request narratives, retention updates.
6. Pricing section: Elite agency tier at $49/month.
7. Final CTA: Start with your next client report.

Trust messages:
- Built for agencies that need faster reporting, not another analytics dashboard.
- CSV-first workflow with no heavy integrations required.
- Fact-Check Lock keeps source numbers unchanged during refinement.
- Local History Vault keeps saved reports on the user's machine.

Tone:
Authoritative, practical, direct, and commercially sharp. Avoid hype. Speak to agency owners who care about time savings, client retention, and operational leverage.

Required page elements:
- Sticky top navigation with logo, Product, Privacy, Pricing, Demo, and CTA.
- Hero with product UI preview.
- Mobile responsive layout.
- CTA buttons that can point to a Stripe Payment Link.
- FAQ with answers about CSV uploads, privacy, data storage, and who the product is for.
- Footer with NarrativeAI, privacy-first reporting, and contact/demo links.
