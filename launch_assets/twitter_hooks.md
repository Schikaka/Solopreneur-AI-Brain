# Twitter/X Launch Hooks

## Privacy-First Thread Blueprint

1. Agencies do not need more dashboards. They need faster client-ready explanations.

2. The hidden time sink in monthly reporting is not pulling the numbers. It is translating them into a credible narrative clients understand.

3. NarrativeAI turns a marketing CSV into a polished executive report in under a minute.

4. The workflow is deliberately simple:
   - Upload CSV
   - Choose the strategic goal
   - Generate the narrative
   - Copy, refine, or save locally

5. Privacy matters. Raw CSV uploads are processed in memory and are not stored on the server.

6. Reports can be saved in the browser's Local History Vault, so agencies keep their own archive on their own machine.

7. The AI output is built around three boardroom-ready sections: what improved, what changed, and what to do next.

8. Best fit: boutique agencies managing paid media, SEO, lifecycle, or performance campaigns for multiple clients.

9. If your account managers spend hours writing report summaries, this is the piece of the workflow I built NarrativeAI to remove.
