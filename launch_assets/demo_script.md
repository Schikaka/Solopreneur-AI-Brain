# 60-Second Demo Script

Boutique agency owners, account directors, and operators who manage recurring client reporting.

## Script

0:00-0:08
"If your agency still spends hours turning marketing dashboards into client report narratives, this is the workflow NarrativeAI removes."

0:08-0:18
"Start with a simple CSV export from your campaign data. No heavy integration setup. No new dashboard for your team to maintain."

0:18-0:30
"Upload the file, choose the strategic directive, and NarrativeAI reads the numbers in memory. It identifies the strongest performance movements, including revenue, spend, ROAS, conversions, and campaign momentum."

0:30-0:43
"Now the report is ready: three executive insights, a boardroom-style narrative, and action recommendations your account manager can use immediately."

0:43-0:52
"The output can be refined in the chatbox while keeping the original CSV numbers locked, so the story gets sharper without changing the facts."

0:52-1:00
"For agencies, the value is simple: faster reporting, cleaner client communication, and less manual writing every month. This is NarrativeAI."
